\begin{tablenotes}\footnotesize
\item Completions: 2,560 GRPO training completions (160 puzzles $\times$ 16, dumped every 20 steps over 400 steps) from a run rewarded by the solver-made-errors judge, so they are not neutral between the two DPO judges. Score: \texttt{mean}, the mean token log-probability of the scored span. Solution + answer is scored after the judge prompt (neutral system prompt and the bare puzzle), the whole completion after the policy's prompt (puzzle and format instructions). Pairs: share of same-puzzle (correct, wrong) pairs ranked correctly (2,736 pairs for solution + answer, 2,895 for the whole completion; ties count 1/2). Groups separated: share of the 81 mixed groups in which every correct completion scores above every wrong one. Base model: Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct. SFT policy: the base model fine-tuned on program-written traces and gold judge-format examples; GRPO's starting point. DPO judges are trained from the SFT policy; solver-made errors: chosen = a correct solution, rejected = a program-written solution wrong in one way (an unavailable number swapped in, a number used twice or added, one false step, or an honest miss of the target); corrupted reasoning: chosen = the policy's solution after its own reasoning, rejected = its solution after empty or truncated reasoning, with no correctness labels.
\end{tablenotes}
