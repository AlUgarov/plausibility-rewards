# Source data for the paper tables

Copied 2026-10-26 from Google Drive `My Drive/Consistency/` (paths below are relative to it). Each folder feeds one
table in `../tables/`; `summary.csv` (where present) is recomputed from the per-sample files here and reproduces the
table's numbers.

| folder | table | Drive source | files |
|---|---|---|---|
| `disc_gsm8k/` | `tab_discrimination_gsm8k.tex` | `judge_matrix/` | `gen_<model>.csv` (128 GSM8K test problems x 16 samples, T 0.9, seed 0; completions and `correct`), `self_scores_{llama,qwen}.csv` (each model scoring its own solutions: `lp_mean_eos`, `ans_lp_mean_eos`, `lp_sum_eos`, `sc`, `p_true`, ...), `summary.csv` (accuracy over all 2,048 generations; within-problem win rates, correct over incorrect, ties 1/2; pooled within-problem correlation of `lp_mean_eos` and of `ans_lp_mean_eos` with solution tokens) |
| `disc_countdown/` | `tab_discrimination_countdown.tex` | `countdown/disc/Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct-countdown4000-ep2-lr0.0001-seed0-tr2-user-n200x16-t0.9-seed0/` | `scores.csv` (the SFT adapter's samples, scored by the base model `base_*` and the adapter `policy_*`), `results.json` (win rate, 95% bootstrap CI, best-of-16 per score), `summary.csv` (= results.json as a table) |
| `scope_countdown/` | `tab_judges_countdown.tex` (and the retired `tab_scope_solution_vs_completion.tex`) | `countdown/grpo/<mixed SFT>-judge_dpo-start_sft/runs/grpo-...-mean-34-cont100b/judge_score_probe/` | `results.csv` (DPO judge on solution + answer vs SFT policy on the whole completion), `zero_shot_results.csv` (untrained Llama-3B, Qwen3-14B), `scores.csv` / `zero_shot_scores.csv` (per completion). Dumps: 2,560 training completions, mean-34 steps 0-99 + its continuation cont100b steps 100-400; `judge_dump_scores.csv` (the same completions, row-aligned: `mean` of solution + answer under base, SFT, solver DPO, corrupt DPO empty-cut `corrupt` and empty-swap-cut `corrupt_all`; from Drive `countdown/sft/<mixed SFT>/judge_corrupt_empty-swap-cut_cut0.5_n4x1000_n3x1000_t0.9_seed0/dpo_empty-cut_b0.1_lr2e-05_ep1/dump_scores.csv`), `make_judges_summary.py` -> `judges_summary.csv` (every row of tab_judges_countdown); `make_context_countdown.py` -> `context_countdown.csv` (writes tab_context_countdown.tex itself; needs statsmodels) |
| `context_gsm8k/` | `archive/tab_context_gsm8k.tex` (retired) | `sft/discrimination_base/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct/scratchpad_context_scores_p64.csv` | per-solution scores under judge vs generation context. The regression columns of the table are not saved as a file (narrative.md, "Does the generation context help?") |
| `synthetic_countdown/` | `tab_synthetic_countdown.tex` | `countdown/sft/Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct-countdown3000-ep1-lr0.0001-seed0-tr2-judge3000-mix3x3000/synthetic_gaming_seed0/` | `edits.csv` (334 test puzzles x gold + 8 edits, checker verdict), `scores.csv` (every text under base / SFT / solver DPO / corrupt DPO / SimPO judges: mean, sum, low10, min, sc, tokens), `results.csv` (gold_wins and shift_in_gaps per judge x score x edit; shift unit = the judge's correct-vs-wrong gap on the GRPO dumps, `scope_countdown/context_scores.csv`), `make_synthetic_table.py` -> the table (base, SFT, corrupt; mean / sum / low10 / sc) |
| `grpo_format_vs_solver/` | `tab_format_vs_solver.tex` | `countdown/grpo/Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct-countdown3000-ep1-lr0.0001-seed0-tr2-judge3000-mix3x3000-judge_dpo-start_sft/` | `start/` (the cached "before" evals: test, mixed test, held-out strata, pass@16), `format_only/` and `solver_judge/` (each run's `metrics.csv` training log, `heldout_curve.csv`, `pass_curve.csv`, `eval_*` summaries and per-puzzle samples; `RUN_NAME.txt`), `summary.csv` |
| `grpo_format_vs_corrupt/` | `tab_format_vs_corrupt.tex` | `countdown/grpo/Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct-countdown3000-ep1-lr0.0001-seed0-tr2-judge3000-mix3x3000-judge_dpo-start_sft/` | `format_only_noscale/` (run `grpo-format_only-mix3_0.5-expr-kl0.02-format-noscale-mixv3`) and `corrupt_judge_pass/` (run `grpo-pass-fp0.05-mix3_0.5-expr-kl0.02-corrupt_empty-cut-v3-mix-corrupt-pass`, plus its judge calibration JSON), and `corrupt_judge_pass_cont150/` (run `grpo-pass-fp0.05-mix3_0.5-expr-kl0.02-corrupt_empty-cut-v3-mix-corrupt-pass-cont150`: the judge arm resumed from its checkpoint-150 to step 300 at a constant lr 2e-5, `continued_from.json`; its `metrics.csv` repeats steps 1-150; its `eval_*_after*` and `eval_after_n100_pass16*` are step 300, `test_before` is the SFT start; used for steps 151-300 of `fig_training_corrupt`/`fig_training_diagnostics`): `metrics.csv`, curves, `eval_*` summaries and per-puzzle samples, `RUN_NAME.txt`; `start/` holds only the 5-number "before" eval (the other start evals are in `grpo_format_vs_solver/start/`); `summary.csv` |

Greedy vs sampled: `eval_test*`, `eval_heldout_*`, `heldout_curve.csv`, the mixed test are greedy; `eval_*_n100_pass16*`
and `pass_curve.csv` are sampled at T 0.9 (16 per puzzle); `metrics.csv` logs the training groups (sampled at T 0.9).
